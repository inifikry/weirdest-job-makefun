# Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown

Entry for the make.fun bounty "Weirdest Job Title: Blind Judge Showdown"
(`u-rusdyxbt-weirdest-job-title-blind-judge-showdown-552d7922`).

`main.py` writes one job title to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Senior Apologizer for Self-Driving Cars

The joke: a senior corporate role whose entire job is apologizing on
behalf of self-driving cars. It reads like a real job title, and the
twist lands on the first read: you can picture the person drafting
apology letters after a robotaxi cuts someone off in traffic.
